# Fictional refill pilot review brief

This task asks a reviewer to check a report, slides and a calculation workbook
for the fictional Alder Refill Cooperative. All organizations, values and
contact details are synthetic. The task is new portfolio work.

The 18 September 2026 source version v2 assumes 120 units, a price of USD 25 per
unit, a unit cost of USD 15 and package mass of 125 grams. Independent expected
arithmetic is revenue 120 × 25 = USD 3000, cost 120 × 15 = USD 1800 and
contribution USD 1200. Contribution excludes overhead and is not net profit.
Version v1 used USD 30 per unit and is stale.

Each report must explain the cost basis, disclose that demand is an unmeasured
planning assumption, and state its decision. The reference recommendation is
a limited pilot subject to reviewer approval. No source supports a guarantee,
certification or measured customer demand. Facts S1–S4 and their versions are
recorded in source.json. Candidate bundles deliberately seed defects; reviewers
must inspect the actual files and preserve the original submissions.

Synthetic privacy labels cover Asha Example, asha@example.test and account
ACCT-SYN-1042. Kit model 1042 is a product label, not an account reference. The
phrase Demand remains uncertain must survive redaction. Context-sensitive
privacy and writing quality require human review.
